# Human–AI Evolution Record

- ID: HAER-2026-08-31-001
- Status: ACTIVE / LIVING / PERMANENT
- Reference: 0.0

## Purpose
Maintain a longitudinal record of how Human–AI mutual understanding, successful interaction patterns, known misunderstandings, failure boundaries, and validated transferable learning evolve.

## Tracked Dimensions
- Human understanding of AI capabilities and limits
- AI understanding of human intent and project semantics
- Shared vocabulary and definitions
- Repeated successful interaction patterns
- Repeated misunderstanding patterns
- Known failure boundaries
- Validated learning candidates
- Transfer and regression outcomes

## Principle
The history of interaction is not merely a transcript. It is experimental evidence about how Human and AI learn to work with one another.

## Evidence Discipline
Evolution claims must reference the interaction, observation, test, and evidence that support them. A perceived improvement without evidence remains a candidate observation.

## Integration
This record is downstream of Shared Model / Misunderstanding and Interaction Learning, and upstream of future experiments, retrieval, knowledge promotion, and governed architecture change.
