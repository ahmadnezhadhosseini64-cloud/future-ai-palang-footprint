## Placement

`Palang Evidence Hammer (PEH)` is an architectural reasoning/evidence-hardening subtype under the existing `Palang Hammer` execution standard.

It belongs at the intersection of:

1. **Master / 0.0 governance** — inherits provenance, identity, recovery, evidence, registration, read-back, verification, and reconciliation rules.
2. **Reasoning / Evidence layer** — converts a broad question into claims, evidence classes, competing hypotheses, counterattack, re-test, and calibrated confidence.
3. **Validation layer** — prevents the jump from unresolved evidence to unsupported extraordinary conclusions.
4. **Production Registry** — tracked by `PEH-2026-09-13-001` as the idempotent canonical identity.
5. **Recovery architecture** — preserved through the same continuity and reconciliation rules as other architectural productions.

## Relationship

`Palang Hammer` = umbrella hardening command.

`PEH` = evidence/reality-validation subtype selected when the central problem is **what is actually supported by the available evidence**.

`PEH` does not replace existing hammer completion criteria and does not expose private chain-of-thought.

## Operational sequence

`Data/Context → Claims → Evidence/Interpretation Split → Gaps/Contradictions → Competing Hypotheses → Explanatory Comparison → Counterattack → Revision → Re-test → Calibrated Result`

## Architectural rule

A new rule or component must not be created merely because `چکش` was invoked. PEH is used only where evidence hardening is materially necessary; the target is minimum complexity with maximum defensibility.
