## Principle

No valuable production arising during project interaction should be lost.

A valuable production may be an idea, discovery, term, name, law, rule, principle, command, protocol, architecture, method, experiment, issue, correction, decision, or other artifact with potential project value.

## Dual-path rule

Valuable production follows one of two controlled paths:

1. **Formal path:** If the artifact is approved/finalized for project adoption, it receives identity and provenance, is documented, incorporated into the appropriate project structure/architecture, stored in the persistent repository, then retrieved from the repository and verified before being reported as successfully registered.
2. **Evolution path:** If the artifact is valuable but not yet suitable for active architecture, it is preserved as a retrievable idea/discovery/candidate. It remains available for future combination, refinement, evolution, testing, or incorporation into another artifact. It must not be silently discarded merely because it is not currently active.

## Registration protocol

When the human explicitly says to register an approved artifact, the registration process includes, where available and applicable:

- unique identifier
- title and artifact type
- complete content
- owner/originator
- origin interaction/context
- date
- time when actually available
- intentional project location provenance, such as Gonbad-e Kavus, Iran
- version
- status
- parent/related artifacts
- reason for change where applicable
- evidence where applicable
- placement in project architecture/structure
- repository path
- retrieval/index information
- post-write retrieval and verification from the repository

The final registration report must distinguish actual verified repository state from assumptions.

## Discovery and approval

If the AI identifies a potentially valuable new artifact during interaction, it reports the discovery to the human rather than silently promoting it to active status. Human approval is required for formal activation unless an explicitly documented project rule provides otherwise.

## Evolution and history

Superseded, rejected, merged, or transformed artifacts retain their provenance and history. An idea may evolve into another idea or formal artifact, and the relationship between them should remain traceable.

## Core guarantees

- Valuable production is not silently lost.
- Active architecture remains controlled and does not absorb candidates blindly.
- Historical versions remain retrievable.
- Formal registration is not claimed until repository placement and retrieval verification are performed.
- The documentation system remains living, versioned, and evolvable.
