## Lesson
A living architecture cannot be a bag of independent documents. Each law, lesson, rule, component, test and gap must keep its own identity, source, version and evidence while being linked to the shared architecture graph and its dependent indexes, queues, tests and recovery paths.

## Operational rule
For every material interaction or new architecture signal:
1. Search existing canonical sources before creating a duplicate.
2. Preserve source provenance and distinguish observation, inference, hypothesis and confirmed rule.
3. Link the new/updated component to its source, master, event, IN/OUT anchors, affected component, acceptance test, index and open issue as applicable.
4. Record lifecycle states separately: REGISTERED, READ-BACK VERIFIED, RECONCILED, VALIDATED, ACTIVE, LIVING and EXECUTION-PROVEN.
5. Keep the last accepted version active within its proven scope while a successor or runtime gap remains OPEN.
6. Evolve through append-only successor versions; preserve failed evidence and rollback lineage.
7. Do not claim automatic capture/enforcement without an implemented runtime and cold-start evidence.
