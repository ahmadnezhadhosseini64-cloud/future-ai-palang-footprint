## Rule Applied

Every recoverable historical archive is evaluated through four primary routes:

- 🟢 **Architectural Value:** genuinely new, validated material suitable for the current architecture.
- 🟡 **Historical / Lineage:** evidence of how an idea, problem, rule, or architecture evolved.
- 🔵 **Candidate Path:** unresolved or future-executable value not yet validated for active architecture.
- ⚪ **Duplicate / Absorbed:** already represented in the current project; preserve provenance without creating duplicate structure.
- ❓ **Unknown / Requires Verification:** insufficient evidence; do not guess.

An archive may legitimately produce more than one route.
