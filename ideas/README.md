# Living Idea & Discovery Archive

This area preserves valuable ideas and discoveries that are not yet active architecture.

## Purpose

An idea must not be lost merely because it is unfinished or not currently suitable for the active architecture. Ideas may later be refined, combined, transformed, tested, rejected, or promoted.

## Lifecycle

`IDEA → CANDIDATE → TESTING → VALIDATED → ACTIVE`

Possible terminal/history states include `REJECTED`, `ARCHIVED`, `SUPERSEDED`, and `MERGED`.

Every promoted artifact should retain a traceable relationship to its originating idea(s) where applicable.
