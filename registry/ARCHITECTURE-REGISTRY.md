## Registration policy

This registry records the active architectural baseline and its historical succession. A new architecture version must preserve the previous version and record the reason, evidence, and relationship that led to the change.

Potential new laws, principles, commands, terms, discoveries, experiments, and architectures detected during interaction are registered as candidates before being promoted to active status.

## Retrieval requirement

Every registered artifact must remain addressable by unique ID and repository path. Superseded artifacts remain retrievable.
