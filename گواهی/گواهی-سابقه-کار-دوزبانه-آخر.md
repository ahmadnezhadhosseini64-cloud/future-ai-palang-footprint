Overseeing flock health, growth and performance, and monitoring rearing indicators, including feed and water consumption, body weight, flock uniformity and mortality; controlling farm environmental conditions and implementing health and biosecurity programs; implementing treatment and vaccination programs in accordance with established plans and instructions; supervising personnel and evaluating their performance; overseeing the collection, grading and dispatch of eggs; preparing and submitting management reports on flock status and treatment measures; evaluating medication effectiveness; and managing the rearing process and preparation of pullets for transfer to the production stage.
